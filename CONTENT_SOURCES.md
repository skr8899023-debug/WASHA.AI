# CONTENT SOURCES

All educational content was authored for this app in simplified educational Arabic and checked
against standard general-chemistry references:

- Atomic structure, bonding, periodic properties: IUPAC definitions and standard secondary-school
  chemistry curricula (atomic number, electron shells 2/8/8 simplification noted as a school model).
- Element data (symbols, categories, common uses): CRC Handbook–level common knowledge; no numeric
  constants are quoted beyond well-known values (e.g., water boils at 100°C at sea level).
- Acids/bases and pH: Arrhenius/Brønsted simplified school definitions; pH scale 0–14 with 7
  neutral (aqueous, 25°C).
- Reactions used in experiments are canonical school examples:
  - 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
  - NaCl dissolution (physical change, dissociation into Na⁺/Cl⁻)
  - HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O (neutralization)
  - CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O (combustion)
- Safety guidance follows common school-lab safety rules (goggles, gloves, supervision, never
  taste/smell directly — waft technique).

Simplifications are intentional and flagged in-app where relevant (e.g., Bohr-style shell model
used for visualization; real orbitals are more complex).
